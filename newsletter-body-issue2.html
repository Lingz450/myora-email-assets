Meet Ifeoluwa, your fitness and movement coach

Her tip this week: move more, not just harder.

If you spend most of your day sitting, don’t wait until your workout to move. Add short movement breaks through the day.

●  Stand up and move
 
●  Take a short walk
 
●  Stretch or change position

These small bursts help reduce the effects of long periods of sitting, keep you feeling less stiff, and make staying active feel more achievable.
