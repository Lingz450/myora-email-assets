Coach’s corner

Esther’s tip for you this week

Your portion matters too.

You don’t always need to remove your favourite foods to eat better. The portion and what you pair them with matter. Try enjoying your usual meals in moderate portions alongside protein and vegetables.

Check out the “Build your plate” guide under the Learn section on the app for more details. More → Learn.
